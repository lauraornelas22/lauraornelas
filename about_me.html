About Me

Professional Background

Hello all! I am a graduate student in the MEITE program at UNC Chapel Hill and a human resources professional with experience in recruitment, HR operations, policy implementation, and organizational development. While my background is not directly in education, I have always found myself in and around the field. I began my career as a college adviser and later worked as an admissions counselor and in program support, where I developed a strong foundation in student services, communication, and supporting individuals through important educational decisions. I have since transitioned into HR and workforce development, expanding my experience in recruitment, employee support, and helping orgeanizations successfully implement and adopt policies and initiatives. I believe both education and HR are fundamentally about learning, developing people, and creating opportunities for growth, and I bring that perspective to my work and studies

Career Aspirations & Goals

My career goal is to leverage my experience in education, recruitment, and human resources to support organizational growth, strengthen workforce development, and create opportunities for individuals to learn, grow, and succeed.

Personal Interests

I have a strong passion for education and expanding access to educational opportunities. Outside of work and school, I enjoy spending time with friends and family, especially sharing a delicious meal and making memories together. I also enjoy traveling and exploring new places.
